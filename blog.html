Why I Started Nova Wallpapers : The Story Behind the Art

A personal note from Nova..

I didn't set out to build a wallpaper business. It started as something much smaller, a habit, really. Late at night, when I couldn't sleep, I'd find myself designing little scenes: a red moon over quiet mountains, two horses standing in still water, a swan curling its neck into something that looked almost like a heart. I wasn't making them for anyone. I was making them because I needed somewhere to put a feeling I didn't have words for.

At some point, I started sharing them on Instagram. Just to see. I wasn't expecting much, maybe a few likes from friends. But people kept coming back. They'd message me asking where a specific piece was from, or tell me a wallpaper matched exactly how they were feeling that week. That part caught me off guard, the idea that something I made in a quiet, private moment could land so specifically in someone else's life, someone I'd never met.

That's really why this site exists. Instagram is wonderful, but it's crowded and fast, and a wallpaper you love gets buried under the next hundred posts within a day. I wanted a place where every design actually gets to stay, organized, easy to find again, easy to download properly instead of screenshotting a blurry version off a story.

Every collection here means something different to me. Crimson Theme is where I go when I want drama, when a feeling is big and I want the whole sky to hold it. Blue Night is the opposite, it's for the quiet nights, the ones where you just want to breathe a little slower. The Islamic collection comes from somewhere even more personal, it's not really about the architecture, it's about how peaceful those spaces feel at night, and I wanted to hold onto that feeling and pass it along.

I'm still learning how to build this site properly, piece by piece, mostly from scratch. If you've downloaded something from here, genuinely, thank you. It means more than you'd probably guess. If a wallpaper ever reminded you of something, or matched a mood you were in, I'd love to hear about it, you can always reach me through the contact page.

More collections are coming. More stories too. Thanks for being here for the beginning of it.

, Nova
