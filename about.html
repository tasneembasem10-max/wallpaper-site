About Me

I'm a digital artist and designer who turns quiet, cinematic moments into wallpapers. I'm drawn to winter light, aurora skies, and glowing night scenes, and I try to give every piece a warmth you can almost feel through the screen, even when the colors are dark.

My style

Most of my work lives somewhere between night and dawn: crimson moons, deep blues, mist, and reflection. I like pairing soft, living subjects, horses, swans, cats, with dramatic skies, so each wallpaper feels like a small story rather than just a background.

Why I make wallpapers

Your phone screen is something you look at dozens of times a day. I want that moment to feel calm and a little beautiful, not just functional. That's the feeling I try to design into every piece here.
